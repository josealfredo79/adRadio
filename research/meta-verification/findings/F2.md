# F2: Common reasons Meta stops sending verification calls

## Findings

### [1] Non-Twilio phone numbers registered with IVR systems cannot receive OTP verification codes
- quote: "If the phone number is registered with an Interactive Voice Response (IVR) system or a computer-operated phone system, you can't receive One-Time Passwords (OTPs)."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/self-sign-up
- source_type: secondary (Twilio official docs, reselling Meta's platform)
- published: unknown (continuously updated)
- confidence: high

### [2] Phone numbers only available for outbound calls/messages cannot receive Meta verification OTPs
- quote: "If the phone number is only available for outbound messages or calls, you can't use it to register a WhatsApp sender because Meta can't deliver OTPs."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/self-sign-up
- source_type: secondary (Twilio official docs)
- published: unknown (continuously updated)
- confidence: high

### [3] Non-Twilio phone numbers must be able to receive SMS or voice calls for WhatsApp verification
- quote: "If the phone number is non-Twilio, it must be able to receive SMS or voice calls."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/self-signup
- source_type: secondary (Twilio official docs)
- published: unknown (continuously updated)
- confidence: high

### [4] VoIP/cloud phone numbers (like Twilio numbers) require a special workaround to receive voice verification codes — voice OTPs cannot be delivered directly to a Twilio number
- quote: For Twilio numbers using voice verification: "configure the phone number to receive the OTP verification code through email" via a Voicemail Twimlet that transcribes incoming calls and emails them. The verification code arrives via email, not as a live voice call.
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/self-sign-up
- source_type: secondary (Twilio official docs)
- published: unknown (continuously updated)
- confidence: high

### [5] A phone number already registered with WhatsApp (consumer or business app) must first be fully deleted/unregistered before it can be used for WhatsApp Business Platform registration
- quote: "If registered with WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app: Delete the WhatsApp account to make the phone number available for the WhatsApp Business Platform with Twilio."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/self-sign-up
- source_type: secondary (Twilio official docs)
- published: unknown (continuously updated)
- confidence: high

### [6] A phone number already registered with another WhatsApp Business Platform provider must have Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) disabled before re-registration
- quote: "If registered with another WhatsApp Business Platform: In the WhatsApp Manager, turn off Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for the number on the WhatsApp Business Platform and register a WhatsApp sender with the number."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/self-signup
- source_type: secondary (Twilio official docs)
- published: unknown (continuously updated)
- confidence: high

### [7] Meta verifies phone number ownership exclusively via SMS or voice call OTPs — there is no alternative verification channel documented for the WhatsApp Business Platform
- quote: "Meta requires you to verify ownership of each phone number before it can send or receive WhatsApp messages. Meta verifies ownership through SMS or voice call OTPs."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/self-sign-up
- source_type: secondary (Twilio official docs)
- published: unknown (continuously updated)
- confidence: high

### [8] Businesses are limited to 2 phone numbers per unverified Meta Business Manager across all WABAs, which may trigger rate limiting or throttling on repeated verification attempts
- quote: "Businesses that don't have a verified Meta Business Manager are allowed a max of 2 phone numbers per Meta Business Manager across all WABAs."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/api#enabling-whatsapp-with-a-twilio-number
- source_type: secondary (Twilio official docs)
- published: unknown (continuously updated)
- confidence: medium (extrapolated — the phone number cap exists, but the exact relationship between hitting the cap and verification call throttling is inferred)
