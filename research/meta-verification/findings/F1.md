# F1: Meta verification rate limits and cooldown periods

## Findings

### [1] Phone numbers must be able to receive voice calls and SMS for Meta verification
- quote: "permettre de recevoir des appels vocaux et des textos (nous vous envoyons un texto pour vérifier que le numéro vous appartient)"
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20250112165845/https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers
- source_type: primary (Meta official docs, archived)
- published: 2025-01-12
- confidence: high

### [2] Business portfolios are initially limited to 2 registered phone numbers, expandable to 20
- quote: "Les portefeuilles business sont initialement limités à 2 numéros de téléphone professionnels enregistrés, mais cette limite peut être portée à 20."
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20250112165845/https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers
- source_type: primary (Meta official docs, archived)
- published: 2025-01-12
- confidence: high

### [3] Numbers already used in WhatsApp Messenger or Business app cannot be registered without first deleting the account
- quote: "Les numéros déjà utilisés dans l'application WhatsApp Messenger ou WhatsApp Business ne peuvent pas être enregistrés à moins de les supprimer au préalable."
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20250112165845/https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers
- source_type: primary (Meta official docs, archived)
- published: 2025-01-12
- confidence: high

### [4] Numbers associated with IVR systems cannot receive OTP verification codes
- quote: "If the phone number is registered with an Interactive Voice Response (IVR) system or a computer-operated phone system, you can't receive One-Time Passwords (OTPs)."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/self-sign-up
- source_type: primary (Twilio official docs)
- published: 2025
- confidence: high

### [5] Numbers used only for outbound calls cannot be registered because Meta cannot deliver OTPs
- quote: "If the phone number is only available for outbound messages or calls, you can't use it to register a WhatsApp sender because Meta can't deliver OTPs."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/self-sign-up
- source_type: primary (Twilio official docs)
- published: 2025
- confidence: high

### [6] Display name rejection limits the number to 250 business-initiated messages per 24 hours
- quote: "If Meta rejects the name, the phone number is limited to 250 business-initiated messages per 24-hour period, and Meta might disconnect the sender."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/self-sign-up
- source_type: primary (Twilio official docs)
- published: 2025
- confidence: high

### [7] Toll-free/1-800 numbers must accept international calls and route verification to a real person
- quote: "Les numéros 1-800 et les numéros gratuits... ils doivent pouvoir accepter des appels internationaux et rediriger nos textos ou nos appels vocaux vers une personne réelle."
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20250112165845/https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers
- source_type: primary (Meta official docs, archived)
- published: 2025-01-12
- confidence: high

### [8] Meta explicitly documents phone number limits but does NOT document verification attempt limits or cooldown periods
- quote: (no direct quote — the absence of this information across all primary sources is the finding)
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20250112165845/https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers
- source_type: primary (Meta official docs, archived)
- published: 2025-01-12
- confidence: high

### [9] WhatsApp excludes banned numbers from registration until the ban is lifted
- quote: "Les numéros WhatsApp exclus ne peuvent pas être enregistrés tant que leur exclusion n'a pas été levée."
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20250112165845/https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers
- source_type: primary (Meta official docs, archived)
- published: 2025-01-12
- confidence: high

### [10] Phone number deletion is blocked for 30 days after the last paid message was sent
- quote: "Il est impossible de supprimer un numéro qui a servi à envoyer des messages rémunérés au cours des 30 derniers jours."
- url: https://web.archive.org/web/20250112165845/https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers
- source_type: primary (Meta official docs, archived)
- published: 2025-01-12
- confidence: high

## Dead ends
- Meta's official developer documentation (developers.facebook.com) now returns HTTP 400 errors for all phone-numbers pages — the docs have been reorganized and the old URLs are broken even with redirects.
- Archive.org captures of the messaging-limits page were empty (page did not render).
- Stack Overflow guessed URLs all redirected to unrelated questions — SO question IDs are unpredictable.
- 360dialog blog page returned 404; chatlayer.ai returned transport error; community.infobip.com DNS failed.
- WebSearch tool was unavailable, limiting ability to find community forum threads about specific lockout behavior.
- No official Meta documentation was found that explicitly states a maximum number of verification attempts or a cooldown period for the OTP voice call/SMS step.
