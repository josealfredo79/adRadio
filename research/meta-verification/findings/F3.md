# F3: VoIP number restrictions for WhatsApp Business verification

## Findings

### [1] WhatsApp's official FAQ explicitly lists VoIP numbers as unsupported for registration
- quote: "Unsupported phone numbers, such as VoIP, landlines, and premium rate numbers, are not eligible for registration."
- url: https://www.whatsapp.com/faq/en/26000023
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (WhatsApp FAQ, accessed July 2025)
- confidence: high

### [2] WhatsApp's verification pipeline classifies numbers via LERG/NPAC carrier databases
- quote: "Carrier database lookup — Checks LERG/NPAC for line type classification. HLR query — Verifies active mobile network registration against a real carrier. Historical abuse check — Numbers with prior spam history are permanently blocked."
- url: https://agentsim.dev/blog/whatsapp-voip-fix
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-03-27
- confidence: high

### [3] Twilio, Vonage, Google Voice, and Bandwidth numbers are all classified as VoIP and blocked by WhatsApp
- quote: "Programmable number blocks appear as VoIP or non-mobile. A programmable number — from Twilio, Vonage, Bandwidth, or AgentSIM — does not have a real mobile carrier registration. It will be classified accordingly."
- url: https://agentsim.dev/blog/whatsapp-voip-fix
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-03-27
- confidence: high

### [4] WhatsApp blocks VoIP numbers specifically for spam prevention and identity verification
- quote: "WhatsApp is the primary communication platform in 180+ countries. They block VoIP and programmable numbers because: Spam prevention: Non-mobile number ranges are statistically far more likely to be used for bulk abuse. Identity verification: Each WhatsApp account is meant to map to one real person or business."
- url: https://agentsim.dev/blog/whatsapp-voip-fix
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-03-27
- confidence: high

### [5] WhatsApp has explicitly blocked VoIP numbers from new registrations and enforced non-mobile verification checks
- quote: "Blocked VoIP numbers from being used for new registrations. Enforced checks that prevent non-mobile numbers from completing verification. Allowed only mobile (non-VoIP) numbers or landlines in some approved regions."
- url: https://www.dataphone.cloud/docs/%E2%9D%8C-whatsapp-business-no-longer-supports-voip-numbers/
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown (2025-era article)
- confidence: medium

### [6] WhatsApp Business via BSP (Business Solution Providers) like Twilio can provision VoIP numbers through the API layer, but the consumer/Business App does not support them
- quote: "BSPs (Business Solution Providers) like Twilio, 360dialog can onboard your number, even VoIP, through the WhatsApp Business API. These providers handle provisioning, hosting, and API integration." and "WhatsApp Business (existing VoIP accounts) — May continue working unless flagged. WhatsApp Business API via BSP — VoIP allowed if provisioned by provider."
- url: https://www.dataphone.cloud/docs/%E2%9D%8C-whatsapp-business-no-longer-supports-voip-numbers/
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown (2025-era article)
- confidence: medium

### [7] Twilio documents WhatsApp Business Platform registration through their console, but does not mention VoIP restrictions explicitly in their API docs
- quote: "To use WhatsApp messaging in production apps, you must enable WhatsApp on your Twilio number. For a step-by-step walkthrough of the process, visit our Self-Signup Guide for WhatsApp."
- url: https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/api
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (Twilio docs, accessed July 2025)
- confidence: high (confirms Twilio numbers can be used as WhatsApp Senders via BSP provisioning)

### [8] Repeated verification attempts with VoIP numbers can permanently flag the number
- quote: "Do not attempt repeated verifications with a VoIP number — this can flag your number permanently."
- url: https://www.dataphone.cloud/docs/%E2%9D%8C-whatsapp-business-no-longer-supports-voip-numbers/
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown (2025-era article)
- confidence: medium

### [9] The HLR (Home Location Register) check confirms no real SIM exists for VoIP numbers
- quote: "HLR (Home Location Register): A real-time query to the mobile carrier's database confirming an active SIM registration. Programmable numbers have no entry here — there is no SIM, no IMSI, no carrier registration. This is why the error message specifically names VoIP: the classification is deterministic from these databases."
- url: https://agentsim.dev/blog/whatsapp-voip-fix
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-03-27
- confidence: high

### [10] Existing WhatsApp Business accounts with VoIP numbers may continue working unless flagged by Meta
- quote: "WhatsApp Business (existing VoIP accounts) — May continue working unless flagged. Do not attempt repeated verifications with a VoIP number — this can flag your number permanently."
- url: https://www.dataphone.cloud/docs/%E2%9D%8C-whatsapp-business-no-longer-supports-voip-numbers/
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown (2025-era article)
- confidence: medium
